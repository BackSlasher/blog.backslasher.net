    Background

    I started in Israeli Navy DevOps, was the first DevOps hire at a martech startup, then spent six years at Facebook on reliability, performance, and security at billion-user scale. After that, architect roles at two growth-stage startups. Independent since 2025.

    I’m self-taught across most of what I do - coding as a kid, sysadmin in high school, scripting in the Navy, Linux when I moved to the startup scene. Twenty years of landing in unfamiliar systems and becoming useful fast.

    CS degree from Open University (with honors).

    I work fixed-price when scope is clear, hourly when it isn’t. Mostly remote.
